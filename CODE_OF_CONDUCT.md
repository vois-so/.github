# Code of conduct

These rules apply to issues, discussions, pull requests and all other areas of this organization.

## Do

- Be polite and patient. Many users are not native English speakers.
- Speak about the work, not about the person.
- Help new users.

## Do not

- Do not attack, insult or threaten a person.
- Do not send sexual content, hate or spam.
- Do not publish personal data of a different person.
- Do not use a voice of a person without the consent of that person.

## What we do

The Vois Studio team removes content that breaks these rules. The team can prevent the access of a person to this organization.

To tell us about a problem, send an email to [support@vois.so](mailto:support@vois.so). Put "Conduct" in the subject. We keep your report private.
